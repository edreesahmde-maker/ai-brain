      Help

      How it works

      1. You type a task in the box in the middle.
      2. Claude (the brain) decides what to do and asks the helper AIs when it is useful. You can watch every message in the helper panels on the left.
      3. Claude checks the answers and gives you one final answer in the middle.

      You can also message a helper directly from its panel, or make pictures in the Image Studio. If you send a task while the brain is busy, it waits in line and runs next.

      Good to know

      • Every task uses your Claude plan limit, and helper calls use your Google plan. The bars in the bottom bar show your Claude limits; the Usage panel shows the details.
      • Work modes (under the task box): Solo is fastest, Team asks Gemini too, Debate has Gemini argue the other side, Extreme plans, drafts and double-checks before answering. Team, Debate and Extreme use more of your limits.
      • Panels: use the buttons on the left edge to show or hide them, drag the edges between them to resize, and use the ⋯ menu on a panel to move it. Your layout is saved.
      • Music controls Spotify through the Windows media controls. Spotify must be open on this PC. Volume is not available.
      • The Discord panel keeps the chats you pin (press + and paste a chat's link). A click opens that chat in the Discord app installed on this PC, next to AI Brain; "Put the windows back" undoes the layout. Discord does not let other apps list your chats, and the AIs cannot see or send your messages.
      • The app keeps running in the background until you press Quit (the power button).
      • The brain can only read files inside its own workspace folder, such as files you attach.
